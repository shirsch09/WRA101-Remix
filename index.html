Girls in middle & high school can spark creativity, confidence, and limitless opportunity through CS.

Why Girls Should Join Computer Science

  • 💡 Gain valuable skills that open doors to high-demand careers.
  • 👩‍💻 Help close the gender gap in tech and inspire the next generation.
  • 🧠 Build creativity, critical thinking, and real-world problem solving.
  • 🚀 Use technology to change the world and solve meaningful challenges.

Computer Science Statistics

“The overall level of representation remains low, despite the importance of diversity in STEM.”

— Women in STEM Statistics

“Women earn only 18% of computer science bachelor’s degrees in the U.S.”

— Women in Computer Science

“Focusing explanations on interests suggests girls are ‘deficient’ — but the real issue is access, culture, and encouragement.”

— Too Few Women in CS & Engineering

How to Get Involved in Computer Science

  • ✨ Join clubs such as Girls Who Code or Women in STEM.
  • 💻 Try beginner-friendly coding platforms like Scratch or Code.org.
  • 🎮 Explore robotics, game design, or web development projects.
  • 📚 Take Computer Science courses at your school to gain foundational skills.
  • 🌟 Attend hackathons or competitions to build experience and confidence.
